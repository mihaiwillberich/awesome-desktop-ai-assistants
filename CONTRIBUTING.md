# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful. This is a community-curated catalog of desktop AI assistants, and additions, corrections, and removals are all welcome.

Please read these guidelines before opening a pull request. PRs that follow the format are usually merged quickly.

## Submission format

Each entry must follow this exact format:

```
- [Product Name](https://producturl.com) - One-sentence description of what it does. *Platforms.*
```

Rules:

- The product name links to the product's official website, not a marketing landing page or affiliate link.
- The description is one sentence, factual, and under 120 characters.
- Platforms are italicized at the end and listed as plain text, e.g. *macOS, Windows, Linux.* or *web, iOS, Android.*
- Entries are added in alphabetical order within their category, unless the category has its own ordering rule documented at the top.
- Do not add badges, emoji, pricing, or screenshots — those belong on the product's own site.

## Neutrality

This list aims to be neutral. We describe what a product does, not what its marketing team wishes you'd say.

Avoid:

- Superlatives ("best", "leading", "revolutionary", "next-gen")
- Comparisons to competitors ("better than X", "the X alternative")
- Marketing slogans copied verbatim from the product's homepage
- Adjectives like "powerful", "seamless", "intuitive", "innovative"

Stick to verbs and nouns: "Generates", "Transcribes", "Summarizes", "Runs locally", "Integrates with Gmail".

## No self-promotion language

Maintainers and people associated with a product may submit it, but submissions must read like any neutral third-party entry. We will edit promotional language out before merging, or ask you to revise.

If you are submitting your own product, disclose this in the PR description. It will not affect whether the entry is accepted, but it speeds up review.

## Proposing a new category

Categories should be broad enough to fit at least three actively-maintained products and distinct enough that products clearly belong in one or the other.

To propose a new category:

1. Open an issue first using the "Add a product" template, explaining the category and listing at least three products that would belong in it.
2. Wait for a maintainer to confirm before opening a PR.
3. In the PR, update both the table of contents and add the new section in the same alphabetical position.

## Link rot policy

Dead links are removed on sight. If you notice a 404 or a domain that's been parked, open an issue or a PR removing the entry. Acquired products that still operate under a new URL should be updated, not removed.

## Inclusion criteria

A product qualifies for the list if:

- It is publicly available (not in private beta or waitlist-only)
- It has a website with documentation and a way to install or sign up
- It is actively maintained — last release or update within the past 12 months
- It is meaningfully AI-powered, not just AI-branded

Discontinued products are removed.
